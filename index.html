Why we exist

Raising the standard of martial arts.

The National Martial Arts Organization exists to professionalize the martial arts — an independent authority that accredits schools, equips them to run, and elevates their students on a national stage. Built by martial artists, for martial artists. We hold that a school which trains with integrity deserves to be formally recognized, and that every family deserves the confidence of a school that has been independently verified.

We commit to three things, and we lead in each: we accredit schools against nine rigorous standards families can independently verify, we equip them with high-value software to run the entire school, and we stage a national championship league that keeps students striving all year long.

Honor the schools. Empower the instructors. Inspire the students.
