01 — Accreditation

Accreditation that actually means something.

Any school can hang a certificate on the wall. NMAO accreditation is earned against nine standards and independently verified — then made public, so prospective families can trust it at a glance.

The nine standards — independently verified
  • A real, established school — registered, accountable, clear mission
  • Verified lineage & credentials — traced, not self-claimed
  • Insured & background-checked — CPR & first-aid ready
  • A safe, monitored space — video & fire safety
  • Qualified instructors — vetted, clear promotion standards
  • Open & transparent — findable, public presence
  • Professional operations — documented, fair, data-protective
  • Clean & health-conscious — cleaning schedule & sanitized gear
  • Held to account — reviewed regularly, revocable
What the school earns
  • The NMAO window seal — with a scannable QR
  • A public verify-a-school page — trust at a glance
  • A national directory listing — stand out to families
  • Over 40 checkpoints — reviewed by a national body
