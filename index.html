01 — Accreditation

Accreditation that actually means something.

Any school can hang a certificate on the wall. NMAO accreditation is earned against nine standards and independently verified — then made public, so prospective families can trust it at a glance.

🛡️

Nine real standards

Safety, lineage, insurance, background checks, operations, hygiene, and more — over 40 checkpoints.

🏅

The NMAO seal

A window decal with a scannable QR that shows prospects exactly what the school has achieved.

🔎

Public verification

Anyone can look up a school and confirm its accreditation is real and current.

📍

National directory listing

Accredited schools stand out to families searching for a place to train.

🧬

Lineage & credentials verified

Instructor rank and lineage are traced and confirmed — not self-claimed.

⚖️

Held to account

Accreditation is reviewed regularly and revocable — it stays meaningful.
