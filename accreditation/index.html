Accreditation

Accreditation that assures legitimacy.

NMAO is the independent, third-party accreditation body for the martial arts. A school earns our accreditation by meeting nine rigorous standards, each verified by NMAO’s accreditation board — and the result is a credential any family can confirm in seconds.

Fully compliant with current legislation. Our accreditation includes the sex-offender-registry background screening now required by law — so becoming accredited keeps your school compliant.

Why accreditation matters

The certificates on your wall already speak to your accomplishment and years of dedication. Accreditation adds a different kind of assurance: an independent review against a published set of standards — confirmed by a national body rather than the school itself, kept current and revocable if those standards ever slip. For a family choosing where to train, it turns a claim into a verified fact — safety, insurance, background-checked instructors, and a documented lineage, each confirmed by an outside authority.

For the school, accreditation is a mark of professionalism that sets you apart — reinforced by a public verification page, so a family checking whether a school can be trusted can confirm yours the moment they look.

The nine standards — verified by NMAO’s accreditation board
  • A legal, established school — registered, accountable, clear mission
  • Verified lineage & credentials — traced, not self-claimed
  • Safe and insured — insured, CPR and first-aid ready
  • A safe, monitored space — video & fire safety
  • Background-checked instructors — vetted and verified
  • Open & transparent — findable, public presence
  • Professional operations — documented, fair, data-protective
  • Clean & health-conscious — cleaning schedule & sanitized gear
  • Held to account — reviewed regularly, revocable
