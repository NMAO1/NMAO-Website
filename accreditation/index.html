Accreditation

Accreditation that assures legitimacy.

NMAO is an independent, third-party accreditation body for martial arts schools. A school earns accreditation by meeting nine standards, verified by NMAO’s accreditation board — and the result is something any family can check in seconds.

Apply for accreditation → See what the badge means

Why accreditation matters

Anyone can print a certificate. Accreditation is different: it is an outside review against a published set of standards, kept current and revocable if a school stops meeting them. For a parent choosing where to send their child, that is the difference between a claim and a verified fact — safety, insurance, background-checked instructors, and a traceable lineage, all confirmed by someone other than the school itself.

For the school, the accreditation window badge is a mark of professionalism that sets you apart — backed by a public verify page and a national directory listing, so the families searching for a trustworthy school can find yours.

The nine standards — verified by NMAO’s accreditation board
  • A legal, established school — registered, accountable, clear mission
  • Verified lineage & credentials — traced, not self-claimed
  • Safe and insured — insured, CPR and first-aid ready
  • A safe, monitored space — video & fire safety
  • Background-checked instructors — vetted and verified
  • Open & transparent — findable, public presence
  • Professional operations — documented, fair, data-protective
  • Clean & health-conscious — cleaning schedule & sanitized gear
  • Held to account — reviewed regularly, revocable
What the school earns
  • The NMAO accreditation window badge — with a scannable QR
  • A public verify-a-school page — trust at a glance
  • A national directory listing — stand out to families
  • Over 40 checkpoints reviewed — by NMAO’s accreditation board
