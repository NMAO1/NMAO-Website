Choosing a school you can trust

For Students and Parents

When a school earns NMAO accreditation, it isn’t just receiving a certificate — it’s earning national recognition as a trustworthy and elite institution. It signals that the school has undergone a thorough review and has met the NMAO standards in areas such as:

  • Instructor background checks
  • Cleanliness and sanitation practices
  • Emergency preparedness
  • Verified lineage and instruction legitimacy
  • Student safety protocols
  • Professional business ethics

Our standards are based on extensive research into martial arts pedagogy, school operations, safety protocol, and traditional weapons training. We’ve built our accreditation criteria with the input of experts across disciplines to ensure that what we offer is both meaningful and respected.

NMAO accreditation is more than a badge — it’s a mark of honor, professionalism, and care. It’s how quality schools differentiate themselves from those who cut corners. And it gives students and families the peace of mind that they are training in a school that truly puts their well-being first.

We are honored to serve the martial arts community, and we are proud to recognize those who meet and uphold the highest standards in the field.
