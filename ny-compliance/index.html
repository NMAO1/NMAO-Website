New York now requires background checks for your school.

A new state law requires martial arts academies — along with dance, gymnastics and other youth programs — to run sex-offender-registry background checks on everyone who works with minors. The bill has passed both houses of the Legislature and awaits the Governor's signature. Here's exactly what it requires, and how NMAO makes compliance effortless.

What the law requires

Under new Section 398-g of the General Business Law, every covered person at a youth-serving facility must be screened against sex-offender registries before working with minors.

The requirements at a glance
  • Three-registry screening — NY State Sex Offender Registry, the federal NSOPW, and any other state lived in over the past 5 years
  • Before they start — screening completed before an operator, employee, contractor or volunteer begins
  • Rechecked every 3 years — compliance must be kept current, not one-and-done
  • Volunteers over 16 hrs/month — or 32 hours per year — are covered too
  • Civil penalty up to $10,000 — per violation, enforced by the Attorney General
  • License on the line — non-compliance can mean suspension or revocation of your permit to operate

The clock starts the day it's signed.

Once the Governor signs, schools have 180 days to comply. The schools that get ahead of it now will be ready — and accredited — before the deadline arrives.
