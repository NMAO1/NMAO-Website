The standard we hold

Code of Ethics

All NMAO accreditation candidates agree to abide by the following Code of Ethics:

  • I shall strive to expand my knowledge within the art and skills I teach.
  • I shall adhere to all safeguards and precautions for the health and well-being of myself, students, and instructors.
  • I shall treat other martial artists with respect and honor, regardless of rank.
  • I shall respect and obey the law, and all regulations within the law, that serve to protect the public regarding the operation of my school.
  • I shall protect the rights and information of my student base.
  • I shall meet and keep to all the standards set forth by the NMAO.

Violation of the NMAO Code of Ethics may be grounds for revoking an accreditation.
