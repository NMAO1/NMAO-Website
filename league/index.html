A league that keeps students coming back.

A nine-round season your students compete in against students from around the nation — monthly tournaments in forms and weapons, head-to-head dueling, leaderboard standings, badges, and collectible medallions — all building to a grand-finale tournament with over $10,000 in prizes available. Competition that fits in a dojo’s week

The hardest part of tournaments has always been the travel, the cost and the logistics. NMAO’s league removes all three: students compete from their own dojo — film an entry, submit it in the Compete app, and certified judges score every round. It keeps students motivated month after month, and it brings revenue back to the school for doing what you already do.

The athlete experience
  • Compete from your own dojo — film it, submit it, done
  • Traditional & creative forms + weapons
  • Certified judges score every entry — 6-criterion rubric, 2 angles
  • Head-to-head dueling — rating-matched, community-voted
  • National skill rating + dueling Elo
  • 100+ badges, living frames & crests
  • Unique collectible 9-piece Medallion — earned across the season
  • Cinematic Monthly Reveal ceremony
  • Leaderboard standings — all season long
  • Enter 5 of 9 rounds to qualify — for the semi-final & grand finale
  • Grand-finale prizes — worth over $10,000
  • Guardian-managed & COPPA-safe
